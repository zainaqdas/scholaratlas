International Students Apartments

Each apartment containsfour-bedrooms and a public bathroom with hot water shower. Every bedroom contains a desk, wardrobe, air conditioner, telephone and network interface, etc. The expenses with telecommunication, internet, bedding, water and electricity charges will be paid by students.

1. Double room: Every person 5000RMB/year

2. Single room: Every person 10000RMB/year

University Hotel

1. Standard single room: 150RMB/Day; Comfortable single room: 200 RMB/Day (private bathroom with hot water shower), bedding, air conditioner, color TV, telephone and Internet access, etc.

2. Standard double room: 180RMB/Day; Comfortable double room: 220 RMB/Day (private bathroom with hot water shower), bedding, air conditioner, color TV, telephone and Internet access, etc.

Board Expenses

About 20RMB/day (about 3USD)in the dining room per student, and about 35RMB/day(about 5USD)in the Muslim dining room for per Muslim student.
