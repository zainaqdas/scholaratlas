    Scholarships

    1. Jiangsu Provincial Government Scholarship (Outstanding students scholarship) 

    Bachelor Students：18000CNY /person

    Postgraduate Students: 30000CNY /person

    2. CZU Undergraduate Scholarship

    (1) Academic Scholarship

    The first-year scholarship will be offered upon the applicants’ successful registration at CZU. Scholarship recipients shall be reviewed in each academic year according to Changzhou University Point-based Management Measures for International Students (Trial). The scholarship shall be suspended or deprived if students fail to pass the annual review. 

    Remarks: accommodation fees are to be paid by the international students.

    Full scholarship: 14000 CNY per person per year 

    Partial scholarship: 9000CNY per person per year

    Application Website: http://cczu.at0086.cn/stuapplication/login.aspx

    Application Date: From February to June

    (2) Individual Scholarship

    There are 6 categories for the scholarship, and international bachelor's students can apply for the scholarship from the second academic year. The scholarship shall be awarded according to Regulations of Individual Scholarship for Undergraduate International Students of Changzhou University (Trial).

    Individual scholarship: 1500CNY/person

    3.CZU Postgraduate Academic Scholarship

    Academic scholarship in the first academic year will be awarded according to the admission conditions. Starting from the second academic year, scholarships will be divided into three grades according to the proportion, and those who fail the review will be cancelled. Scholarships shall be granted in accordance with Regulations of Academic Scholarship for Postgraduate International Students of Changzhou University.
