ZJUT Full Scholarship

In order to further enhance the internationalization of our university, attract more international students to study and engage in scientific research, foster a positive and proactive academic atmosphere among international students, and contribute to the development of our university's advantageous disciplines and its status as a "Double First-Class" institution, we are implementing a pilot reform for the doctoral new student scholarship based on the existing "Regulations for the Evaluation of New Student Scholarships for International Students at Zhejiang University of Technology (Revised)."The specific evaluation criteria for the doctoral new student scholarship are as follows:


Eligible Programs：PhD Programs


Types of Scholarships

  • ZJUT PhD Full Scholarship: Covers tuition fees, accommodation expenses, and a monthly living allowance of 3,500 RMB per person. The scholarship duration aligns with the program duration specified in each major's training plan. During any extended period beyond the regular graduation timeline, students are responsible for their own expenses, and the university will no longer provide scholarship funding. Accommodation standards are based on double room.

  • ZJUT PhD Scholarship: Covers tuition fees, accommodation expenses. Monthly living allowance is NOT included for this type of scholarship. The scholarship duration aligns with the program duration specified in each major's training plan. During any extended period beyond the regular graduation timeline, students are responsible for their own expenses, and the university will no longer provide scholarship funding. Accommodation standards are based on double room.


Scholarship quota

  • 3 ZJUT PhD Full Scholarships

  • Unlimited ZJUT PhD Scholarships


Application Deadline

End of May


Eligible Disciplines for Scholarship Application

All relevant doctoral programs covered by the university's existing advantageous disciplines.


Scholarship Evaluation Criteria

1. Average scores during the master's degree stage should generally be above 85.

2. Graduation from a university ranked within the top 600 globally.

3. Outstanding research capabilities, including the publication of SCI or EI indexed papers during the graduate period.

4. Priority given to applicants for Chinese-taught programs.


Continuation Criteria for Scholarship

1. Chinese-taught students must achieve a GPA of 2.5 or higher.

2. English-taught students must achieve a GPA of 3.0 or higher.

3. Attendance rate of 90% or higher, with no violations of national laws, regulations, or university rules.

4. Must receive an "Excellent" rating in the annual evaluation by the supervisor.

5. Starting from the second academic year, scholarship recipients must participate in the annual defense review (non-participation will be considered automatic forfeiture). Emphasis will be placed on evaluating research progress, and the scholarship committee will decide whether to continue providing financial support.

6. In the third year, students must publicly publish (including acceptance) at least one SCI or EI indexed paper as the first or second author.


Application Procedure

  1. Students need to Log into our official application website:

    https://zjut.at0086.cn/StuApplication/Login.aspx

    Please choose yes when you see the question“whether to apply for a scholarship”at the page of application information：

  2. If the applicant accepts the pre-admission offer and the scholarship category, a non-refundable application fee of RMB 400 must be paid as instructed and the payment proof uploaded to the application system to secure your seat.

  3. The scholarship certificate will be uploaded to the application system; the original can be collected at registration.

  4. Scholarship recipients should register on time with the admission letter/scholarship certificate and other necessary materials;

  5. After registration, Scholarship recipients should apply for the ICBC bank card and send the bank

    card information to the email: ischolarship@zjut.edu.cn before October 31; 

  6. After receiving the ICBC bank card, scholarship recipients need to sign a bank withholding

     agreement, which authorizes the school to deduct the amount of tuition fee directly from the   

     student's ICBC card after the scholarship is issued.
