I. Application requirements

1. The applicant must not be a citizen of the Peopleâs Republic of China and must be in good health.

2. Academic requirements for the applicant:

a.Chinese language learner: must have received high school education or equivalent or higher education;

b.Undergraduate: must have received high school education or equivalent or higher education;

c.Post-graduate: must have received undergraduate education or equivalent or higher education;

d.PhD: must have received post-graduate education or equivalent or higher education;

e. Applicants for undergraduate, post-graduate, and PhD courses must reach Band 4 in the new HSK test; those who have not yet reached Band 4 can take the language preparatory course first, and then transfer to his or her major class after qualifying.

II. Time of admission:

Students applying for courses which do not provide a diplomacan enter at any time;

Students applying for courses with a diplomamust be enrolled before September 1st.

III. Length of schooling and tuition fee

Registration fee: 800 yuan.

Non-diploma courses: No limitation on the length of schooling. Tuition fee of 3,500 RMB/month, 9000 RMB /3 months, 16,000 RMB/year (10 months);

Undergraduate: 4 years of schooling. Tuition fee of 16000 RMB/year for Chinese teaching, and 20000 RMB/year for English teaching;

Post-graduate: 2-3 years of schooling. Tuition fee of 20,000 RMB / year for Chinese teaching, and 24,000 RMB / year for English teaching.

PhD: 3 years of schooling. Tuition fee of 30,000 RMB / year for Chinese teaching, and 38,000 RMB / year for English teaching.

IV. Boarding fee

30 RMB / day for a single room (indoor toilet; Air conditioning, TV, refrigerator, shared kitchen, shared laundry room), RMB 9000 / year;

20 RMB/day for a double room (indoor toilet; Air conditioning, TV, refrigerator, shared kitchen, shared laundry room), 6000 RMB/year.

V. Scholarship of Northeast Petroleum University for International Students

First-class scholarship: Each individual scholarship is 11,000 RMB/Year

Second-class scholarship: Each individual scholarship is 8,000 RMB/Year

Third-class scholarship: Each individual scholarship is 7,500 RMB/Year

VI. Application materials required:

1. Two copies ofNortheast Petroleum University Application for Admission-Foreign Students(which can be downloaded fromhttp://gjjy.nepu.edu.cn/, or obtained from School of International Education of Northeast Petroleum University);

2. A copy of the applicantâs passport;

3. Students applying for a diploma must provide a copy of their high school diploma and HSK certificate; those applying for master or doctoral degrees should also provide their undergraduate diploma, and post-graduate diploma;
