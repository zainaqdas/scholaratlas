Notice on Application for Zhejiang Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students 2025

Zhejiang Provincial Government Scholarship for International Students is a special scholarship launched by Zhejiang provincial government, which aims to attract excellent international students to study in universities or colleges in Zhejiang Province. Please pay attention to the following information if you want to apply:

1.Object: all international self-funded students at ZAFU

2.Eligibility:

a. Non-Chinese citizens in good health conditions;

b. Preservation of friendly attitudes towards China and compliance with laws and regulations in China as well as rules and disciplines of ZAFU;

c. Evidence of proficiency in Chinese (Priority will be given to applicants with HSK4 certificates);

d. High attendance rate and excellent academic performance. GPA(academic year of 2024-2025) > 3.0. No re-taking or failed courses;

e. Active participation in Chinese cultural activities at ZAFU. (Students who make special contributions to the management or development of the International College will be given special consideration or top priority. )

f. Compliance with relevant regulations of Chinese government and ZAFU. No occurrence of the delay of daily report or fingerprint attendance.

3. The application should be delivered between June 30th and September 16th 2025

4.Documents required for application:

a. Application Form (see the attachment);

b. Copy of passport and valid visa;

c. Medical report (done after September 2024);

d. Academic transcript of the academic year of 2024-2025( GPA> 3.0);

e. Attendance sheet of the academic year of 2024-2025;

f. HSK report (above HSK 3, HSK 4 preferred);

g. Other supporting documents such as honorary certificates (with counselor's signature)


Please submit your application to Room 515 in International College Building on workdays from June 30th to September 16th.

5.Procedures:

Results will be publicized on college website after all applications are reviewed by the scholarship assessment team of International College. Please feel free to contact Mrs He at 63926895 if you have any questions.

College of International Education (Confucius Institute)
