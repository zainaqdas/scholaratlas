Chinese Government Scholarship Program

Application

Applicants should apply through the official website of Chinese Scholarship Council and the School of International Education and Exchanges (hereinafter referred to as SIEE) of NEFU between November and March.


Eligibility

1. Applicants must be non-Chinese nationals in good health;

2. Education background required and age limit;

a) Applicants for bachelor degree studies must have graduated from high school and be under the age of 25;

b) Applicants for master degree studies must have bachelorâs degree and be under the age of 35;

c)Applicants for doctoral degree studies must have masterâs degree and be under the age of 40;

3. Language requirements:

a) For Chinese-taught programs, applicants should pass HSK Test Level 4.

b) For English-taught programs, bachelor degree applicants should get no less than 6.0 IELTS or 90 in TOEFL; master or doctor degree applicants should get no less than 6.5 IELTS or 95 in TOEFL.

4. Excellent results in study;

5. Applicants should have good ability in scientific research;

6. The scholarship cannot be combined with any other scholarship.


Application Documents

The applicants must fill in and provide the following materials truly and correctly (in duplicate):

1. Application Form for Chinese Government Scholarship. Those who are available for online application shall fill in and print the application form after submitting online.

âThe CSC Online Application System for Study in China is available at http://www.csc.edu.cn/studyinchina

Please choose the Program Category Type B

NEFU Agency code: 10225

2. Applicants should log into https://istudy.nefu.edu.cn/, submit their applications and print NEFU application form.

3. Highest diploma (notarized photocopy). If applicants are university students or already employed, they should provide pre-graduation certificate or employment certificate.

4. Transcripts (notarized photocopy in English or Chinese).

5. HSK Certificates (valid within 2 years) or English level certificates such as TOEFL or IELTS.

6. A study or research plan (no less than 1000 words).

7. Two recommendation letters by professors or associate professors.

8. Passport copy.

9. Photocopy of Foreigner Physical Examination Form.

The medical examinations must cover all the items listed in the Foreigner Physical Examination Form. Incomplete records or those without the signature of the attending physician, official stamp of the hospital or a sealed photograph of the applicants are invalid. The medical examination result is generally valid for 6 months.

10. Certificate of Clearance.

11. Financial Guarantee.


Details of the Scholarship

1. Exempt from tuition fee, on-campus accommodation fee and application fee;

2. CNY 800 per year for Comprehensive Medical Insurance;

3. Monthly living allowance is granted to the students at the following rates (CNY Yuan per month):
