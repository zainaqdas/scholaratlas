Launch your future in Australia 

Adelaide University is the place to realise your ambitions. A purpose-built, future focused university where you can be inspired by your environment and your peers. We're delivering high-quality teaching alongside an exceptional student experience, ensuring you benefit from our research excellence, industry partnerships, and state-of-the-art facilities.  

Set your future in motion – study in one of the world’s most liveable cities and graduate ready to create a brighter future.
