Earn an Online American University Degree, Tuition-Free

Last updated: 20 Jan 2015 | SPONSORED POST

If you’re interested in earning an American university degree, consider University of the People. UoPeople is a non-profit, tuition-free, online, accredited university dedicated to opening access to higher education globally.

University of the People does not charge students to take classes, does not charge for any reading and other study materials, and does not charge for annual enrollment. In order to remain sustainable, UoPeople charges small processing fees for application and examination processing. However, for those individuals who are unable to pay these nominal processing fees, UoPeople has a variety of scholarship funds designed to meet the unique needs of its students.

UoPeople offers associate and bachelor’s degrees in business administration and computer science. As a student at UoPeople, you will learn in small online classes with personal attention from world-class professors. Your classmates will be a diverse group of students who come from all over the world.

UoPeople prepares its students for employment by offering internship and mentoring programs to gain hands-on experience with its partners such as HP and Microsoft; in addition to academic study of the industry.

With UoPeople you can study anytime, anywhere, tuition-free!

 

Learn more about what it’s like to study at UoPeople and what makes our students so happy in this video.  To learn more about UoPeople’s mission, its unique ultra-low-cost model, and how it works, watch the TED Talk delivered by the university’s President, Shai Reshef (over 2.5 million views so far!) – click here.
