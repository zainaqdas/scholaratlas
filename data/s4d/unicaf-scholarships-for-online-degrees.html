7 reasons why you should apply to unicaf right now!

  1. Flexibility: Study online through our flexible delivery model without neglecting your job or family.
  2. Affordability: Receive a generous scholarship and pay the remaining balance in small monthly instalments. More than $100 million worth of Unicaf scholarships have been awarded to over 80,000 students around the world so far.
  3. Internationally recognised qualifications: Earn a degree from one of our British university partners (The University of Suffolk, the University of East London) or from the multi-campus Unicaf University accredited locally and by the British Accreditation Council (BAC).
  4. Programmes: Wide range of Bachelor, Master's, and Doctoral (Unicaf University only) degrees, as well as professional short courses.
  5. Multiple start dates: Multiple start dates during the year.
  6. Pan-African: With state-of-the-art campuses and learning centres in 8 African countries and more opening soon.
  7. Global networking: Learning becomes exciting. Network with students from 170 countries.
