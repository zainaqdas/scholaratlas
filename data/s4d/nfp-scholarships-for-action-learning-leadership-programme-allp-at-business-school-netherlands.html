NFP Scholarships for Action Learning Leadership Programme (ALLP) at Business School Netherlands

Last updated: 29 Jan 2015 |

Business School Netherlands
Diploma 

Deadline: check website
Study in:  Netherlands
Course starts August 2015

Brief description: [ad#contentadtext]

The Action Learning Leadership Programme comprises of the first phase of the International Action Learning MBA, BSN’s “flagship” Masters programme awarded with American (ACBSP) and Swiss (eduQua) accreditation status. Students will receive the first phase certificate of the MBA.

Participants will focus on improving their decision-making, creativity and other managerial skills, while learning to analyse their own organisation, develop inspiring new insights and finalise a development plan for themselves as well as their own organisation.

Host Institution(s):

Business School Netherlands is a registered Dutch University of Applied Sciences.

Field of study:

Diploma in Leadership, Business Management, Business Administration, Organisational Management, Strategic Management, Entrepreneurship

Number of Awards:

Not specified

Target group:

Highly motivated international managers and (trainee) managers living and working in one of the 51 NFP countries.

Scholarship value/inclusions:

For successful applicants, the NFP scholarship will cover the full tuition fee, international flights and accommodation while at the international conferences, local transfers, meals and prescribed text books.

Eligibility:

Who can apply?

•   Managers and entrepreneurs who want to develop themselves and their company/ institution

•   Be in a management or supervisory position

•   Hold a Bachelor’s degree or equivalent diploma

•   Have a minimum of two years appropriate and relevant post graduate work experience

•   Should the applicant’s mother tongue not be English he/she will require a TOEFL or IELTS language test result. The minimum pass mark for TOEFL is 550 (paper); 213 (computer) or 79/80 (internet based) and IELTS 6.0.

Applicants without a Bachelor’s degree can be accepted when they have at least six to seven years working experience at Bachelor’s level.

Application instructions:

It is important to visit the official website (link found below) or contact them directly at international[at]bsn.eu. for detailed information on how to apply for this scholarship and to access the online application form.

Website:

Official Scholarship Website: http://www.bsn.eu/internationalmba/leadership-course/leadership-course.html
