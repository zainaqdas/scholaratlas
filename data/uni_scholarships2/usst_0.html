Undergraduate Graduate Non-degree Education Scholarship

Prerequisites of the applicants

(1)Non-Chinese citizen in good health.

(2)Applicants pursuing Bachelor's degree should hold High School degree diploma or the equivalent and should be under the age of 25. In addition, the applicant can only apply for SGS Type B scholarship.

Applicants pursuing Master's degree should hold Bachelor degree diploma or the equivalent and should not be over the age of 35.

Applicants pursuing Doctor's degree should hold Master degree diploma or the equivalent and should not be over the age of 40.

(3)The applicants should have excellent academic records.

(4)The applicants should be in default of any other Chinese government scholarship.
