(Exceptions for cases receiving the National Scholarship (type 1) and part of the Low Income Family Scholarship) Provide a scholarship corresponding to school support fees for 1 year (2 semesters) after admission regardless of academic record. Provide a scholarship corresponding to school support fees if the student has completed at least 12 credits in the previous semester and maintained a cumulative GPA of B- (3.0/4.3). Students receiving National Scholarship and External Scholarships must fulfill the academic requirements of the beneficiary institutions (even if the academic requirements of the beneficiary institutions are not met, a financial aid scholarship is provided if the above 2) and 3) requirements are fulfilled). A scholarship is provided if the student graduates within 8 semesters after admission or fulfills the above requirements. For students enrolled after 2007, implement beginning in the Fall semester of 2011. The previous semester’s grades are applied beginning with the Spring semester of 2011. Students taking a leave of absence in the Spring semester of 2011 will only receive a scholarship if they were a scholarship recipient at the time. Students studying abroad without taking a leave of absence in the Spring semester of 2011 will base their scholarship eligibility on the academic record of the semester before studying abroad. Provide scholarship according to previous scholarship system for students enrolled before 2006.
