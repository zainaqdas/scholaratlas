		<h4><strong>Universitat de Val&egrave;ncia - AUIP Scholarships 2026-2027</strong></h4>

<h6><strong>Call</strong></h6>

<ul>
	<li><a href="https://www.uv.es/coopweb24/2026/AUIP/2026_06_10_-resolucion-provisional-becas-master-uv-auip-2026-2027_val_esp%20c%c3%b2pia.pdf">Final Results&nbsp;</a>(<a href="https://webges.uv.es/uvTaeWeb/CambiarLenguajeDoAction.do?idioma=en&amp;servicioNavegacion=-1&amp;grupoNavegacion=-1">UV Notice Board 15/06/2026</a>)</li>
	<li><a href="https://www.uv.es/coopweb24/2025/AUIP/Convocatoria-ayudas-master-uv-auip-2025-26-cas%20c%c3%b2pia.pdf" target="_blank">Call for applications and&nbsp;</a><a href="https://www.uv.es/coopweb24/2026/AUIP/2026-02-10-convocatoria-ayudas-master-uv--auip-2026-2027_1VRA%20c%c3%b2pia.pdf">list of available Master&#39;s&nbsp;degrees</a></li>
	<li><a href="https://webges.uv.es/uvTaeWeb/MuestraInformacionEdictoPublicoFrontAction.do?accion=inicio&amp;idEdictoSeleccionado=97786">Publication on the Notice board of the UV</a> (31/03/2026)</li>
	<li><a href="https://dogv.gva.es/es/resultat-dogv?signatura=2026/9437">Publication on&nbsp;the DOGV</a> (31/03/2026)</li>
</ul>

<p>Application deadline:<strong>&nbsp;from 1 April 2026 to 24 April 2026</strong></p>

<h6><strong>Object</strong></h6>

<p>The University of Valencia (UV), in collaboration with the <a href="https://www.auip.org/es/">Association of Ibero-American Postgraduate Universities (AUIP)</a>, offers 12 scholarships for students from Latin American universities that are AUIP member institutions to pursue an official Master&rsquo;s degree program at the University of Valencia.</p>

<p>There are two types of these scholarships:&nbsp;</p>

<ul>
	<li>
	<p><strong>8 new awards</strong> for pursuing one of the official master&rsquo;s degrees at the University of Valencia (UV), as listed in Annex II of the call.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>4 renewal awards</strong> for the continuation and completion of studies in the official master&rsquo;s programs at the UV during the 2026&ndash;27 academic year, for programs lasting more than 60 ECTS credits.</p>
	</li>
</ul>

<h6><strong>Requirements</strong></h6>

<p><strong>For new grants:</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Be a graduate of a university in a Latin American country that is a member of the AUIP.</li>
	<li>Be a new student at the University of Valencia (UV).</li>
	<li>Not have resided in Spain for more than twelve months within the three years before the date of the call for applications.</li>
	<li>Meet the general requirements for admission to official master&rsquo;s degree programs in Spain, as well as the specific academic requirements of the master&rsquo;s program for which the grant is requested.</li>
	<li>Have a minimum grade point average of 8 (on a 10-point scale), certified in a document showing the equivalence in accordance with the Spanish grading equivalency procedure.</li>
	<li>Not have received this grant in the 2025&ndash;2026 academic year. For this purpose, both students who actually received the grant and those who formally declined it will be considered beneficiaries.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>For renewal grants:</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Have been enrolled in one of the following official master&rsquo;s programs at the University of Valencia (UV):
	<ul>
		<li>Master&rsquo;s Degree in International Economic Management: Management of International Trade</li>
		<li>Master&rsquo;s Degree in Planning and Management of Business Processes</li>
		<li>Master&rsquo;s Degree in Psychological Intervention in Social Settings</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li>Have been a beneficiary of one of the AUIP scholarships offered by the University of Valencia&rsquo;s Vice-Rector&rsquo;s Office for Sustainability, Cooperation, and Healthy Living during the 2025&ndash;2026 academic year.</li>
</ul>

<h6><strong>Financial endowment</strong></h6>

<p>The grants for both modalities will be as follows:</p>

<ol>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Tuition fees:</strong> the amount of ECTS credits for the Master&#39;s subjects, up to a maximum of 60 ECTS. This does not include administrative fees for pre-registration, diploma issuance fees, or other fees or insurance.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Global financial aid of 2.500 euros:</strong>&nbsp;it will be paid in two installments to the student by AUIP into the bank account opened by the beneficiary in Spain.</p>
	</li>
	<li>
	<p><strong>Full-board accommodation and meals</strong> at the College of Residence are provided by the University of Valencia during the 2025-2026 academic year.</p>
	</li>
</ol>

<p>More information and how to apply:&nbsp;<a href="https://www.auip.org/es/becas-auip/2920-becas-auip305" target="_blank">https://www.auip.org/es/becas-auip/2920-becas-auip305</a></p>

<p>It is necessary to previously register as a user.</p>

<p>The system will send a confirmation email to the interested person&#39;s email account with a link to continue the registration process. Then you must follow the steps indicated by the same application. You must consult the documentation and other procedures in the call.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Students applying for renovation are not required to submit additional documentation, but they do have to submit an application.
