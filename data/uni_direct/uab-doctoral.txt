							Grants, Scholarships and Calls
						</h1><div class="cos-text"><p>On this page you will find information about scholarships.</p>

<p>Specifically, these are the most important predoctoral scholarships that are managed by the Research Area of the UAB and that are disseminated through our distribution lists when they are announced.</p>

<p>If you are interested in receiving the information, you can contact&nbsp;<a href="mailto:recerca.respon@uab.cat" target="_blank">recerca.respon@uab.cat</a>&nbsp;to be included in the distribution list, indicating which field you want to receive information from among the following (you can request to receive information from more than a scope):</p>

<ul>
	<li>Social</li>
	<li>Humanities</li>
	<li>Social and Humanities</li>
	<li>Health</li>
	<li>Experimental</li>
	<li>Health and Experimental</li>
	<li>Engineering</li>
</ul>
