<div class="contents_designer-contents headline_box undisplay-m"><h2>Financial Aid</h2></div>
<div class="contents_designer-contents headline_box undisplay-m"><h3>Enrollment Fee Exemption and Deferment program /Tuition Fee Exemption program</h3></div>
<div class="contents_designer-contents text_one undisplay-m"><div class="float-box">
    <div class="text-area">
<p>There is a program for those who fall under the following application qualifications to allow exemption of enrollment fee and tuition fee or to allow deferment the enrollment fee after selection based on each application. In principle, it is categorized as follows, a half enrollment fee exemption and a full/half/quarter-tuition fee exemption.</p>
<p><strong><a href="/ja/education/fees/exempt01/">・Enrollment Fee Exemption Program</a></strong><br>(a) Incoming post-graduate students who are recognized by the university as exhibiting outstanding academic performance, but cannot afford to pay the enrollment fee because of their economic circumstances, and (b) incoming undergraduate students under extenuating circumstances may apply for full or half exemption of the enrollment fee.</p>
<p><strong><a href="/ja/education/fees/grace/">・Enrollment Fee Deferment Program</a></strong><br>Incoming students who cannot afford to pay the enrollment fee by the deadline because of their economic circumstances and who are recognized by the university as exhibiting outstanding academic performance and incoming students under extenuating circumstances may apply for deferment of the enrollment fee. Deferment of the enrollment fee is a postponement of the payment period and not an exemption of the enrollment fee and, therefore, the full amount of the enrollment fee must be paid.</p>
<p><strong><a href="/en/education/fees/exempt02">・Tuition Fee Exemption Program</a></strong><br>Graduate students who are recognized by the university as exhibiting outstanding academic performance, but cannot afford to pay the tuition fee because of their economic circumstances may apply for half, full and quarter exemption of the tuition fee.</p>
</div>
</div></div>
<div class="contents_designer-contents headline_box undisplay-m"><h3>Scholarships for International Students</h3></div>
<div class="contents_designer-contents text_one undisplay-m"><div class="float-box">
    <div class="text-area">
<p><strong><a href="https://www.isc.kyushu-u.ac.jp/intlweb/en/student/government-expense" target="_blank">Japanese Government (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship</a></strong> <br> This scholarship program was established by the Government of Japan in order to promote an acceptance of international students from all over the world. There are following three categories:<br> (1) by embassy recommendation, (2) by Kyushu University recommendation(overseas applications), and (3) through Kyushu University nomination (domestic applications by those enrolled at Kyushu Univ.) <br>Please note that Chinese students have different procedures.</p>
<p><strong><a href="https://www.isc.kyushu-u.ac.jp/intlweb/en/admission/school-fees#school-fees_2" target="_blank">Kyushu University Friendship Scholarship</a></strong><br> Under Kyushu University Friendship Scholarships Scheme (hereafter Friendship Scholarship), Kyushu University invites students from abroad who attend prestigious universities in their home country and who will play an important role in their nation's future. <br> By offering them a chance to study abroad at Kyushu University, the scheme aims to foster the nation's future leaders, maintain good relations with Kyushu University, and play the role of point of contact to cultivate joint research and bring in external capital depending on their course or career after graduation.</p>
<p><strong><a href="https://www.isc.kyushu-u.ac.jp/intlweb/en/student/page-012#student_p12_3" target="_blank">JASSO (Japan Student Services Organization) Scholarship</a></strong><br> This scholarship is available for exchange students from our overseas partner universities. Since application for JASSO should be done through Kyushu University, indicate so on the application form of Kyushu University Student Exchange Program when you would like to apply for it.</p>
<p><strong><a href="https://www.isc.kyushu-u.ac.jp/intlweb/en/student/page-012" target="_blank">Scholarships for Self-supporting International Students</a></strong><br> Scholarship information for self-supporting international students is available on <a href="http://www.isc.kyushu-u.ac.jp/intlweb/scholarship/view/list.php?nendo=2016&amp;lang=en">our website.</a></p>
</div>
</div></div>
<div class="contents_designer-contents headline_box undisplay-m"><h3>Kyushu University Scholarships and Financial Support</h3></div>
<div class="contents_designer-contents text_one undisplay-m"><div class="float-box">
    <div class="text-area">
<p><strong>Yamakawa Prize and Grant (Kyushu University Fund)</strong> <br> This grant is named after the first president of the university, Kenjiro Yamakawa. It is granted for undergraduate students who have excellent academic record and excellent aspiration for humanity, sociability, internationality and expertise.</p>
<p><strong>Grant for Students' Unique Studies and Social Activities (Kyushu University Fund)</strong> <br> This is granted to support the cost of unique studies and social activities which are planned by students or research students.</p>
<p><strong>Grant for Students’ Participation in International Conferences (Kyushu University Fund)</strong> <br> This is granted to support the cost of travel for students to participate and present projects and research results at international conferences.<br>Grant for Research Activities (Kyushu University Student Support Association) <br> This is granted to graduate students who have excellent academic record and whose parent is or will be a member of Kyushu University Student Support Association.</p>
<p><strong>Emergency Grant (Kyushu University Student Support Association)</strong> <br> This grant is given for students, whose parent is or will be a member of Kyushu University Student Support Association, struggling with serious financial circumstances due to family emergency, crisis or death.</p>
