Duration: from a minimum of 6 months to a maximum of 12 months. Recipients: Sapienza Master's degree graduates or single-cycle master's degree or equivalent. Scholarship: € 1,290.00 per month Scholarships for attendance of courses or advanced training activities are exempt from Irpef treatment pursuant to Article 4 of Law 476/84.
