				<div class="txt_con">
					<ul class="list_01">

 - TOPIK Level 6 or Completion of Level 6 at SLI: 100% tuition waiver<br>

								<p class="info_txt">※ Scholarship shall be provided even if the successful candidate additionally meets the above requirements after enrollment and before the beginning date of the semester / Among successful candidates, those recommended by SLI may receive the scholarship</p></li>
					</ul>

				<div class="txt_con">
					<ul class="list_01">

						<li><span class="s_tit">Qualification and Amount</span><br>
 -  IELTS 8.0 / TOEFL iBT 110 or higher : 100% tuition waiver<br>
  - IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL iBT 95 or higher : 50% tuition waiver
								<p class="info_txt">※ Scholarship shall be provided even if the successful candidate additionally meets the above requirements after enrollment and before the beginning date of the semester.</p>
						</li>
					</ul>
