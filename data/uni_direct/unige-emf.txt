<h1 class="o-Title--h1 u-mt0">Excellence Master Fellowships</h1>

    <p>The Faculty of Science of the University of Geneva is an internationally&nbsp;recognized leading research institution. This cutting-edge research covers a broad range of scientific disciplines: astronomy, biology, chemistry and biochemistry,&nbsp;computer science, mathematics, physics, pharmaceutical sciences, earth sciences.</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p>The Faculty hosts two National Centres of Competence in Research of the highly competitive Swiss National Sciences Foundation (<a href="http://www.nccr-swissmap.ch" target="_blank">SwissMAP</a>; <a href="http://www.nccr-planets.ch" target="_blank">Planets</a>)&nbsp;as well as many European research projects. The Masters of the Faculty of Science constitute an ideal training base for an academic but also a professional development, opening on careers in industry, teaching, administration in sciences, communication and media... The programs Master&#39;s degrees are either entirely in English, or in English and French (<a href="https://www.unige.ch/sciences/en/enseignements/formations/masters" title="Masters">see the descriptions of each program</a>).</p>

<p>The Faculty of Science, with the support of external partners, has established an Excellence Fellowship program open to outstanding and highly motivated students who wish to pursue a Master of Science degree in one of the disciplines covered by the Faculty<sup>1</sup>. Candidate selection is based on excellence.</p>

<p><strong>The Excellence Fellowship consists of a grant amounting to CHF 10&#39;000 to CHF 15&rsquo;000/year (no teaching duties); it is awarded for one year and extended for the regular duration of the chosen Master&#39;s program (three or four semesters) provided the applicant is academically successful at the end of his/her first semester of studies.</strong></p>

<hr />
<p>Regardless of your home university, whether you have passed or are doing a brilliant bachelor degree, and that you are one of the best in your year<sup>2</sup>, this program is for you! You must meet the admission criteria of the Master of your choice<sup>3</sup> and be selected on the basis of the application file for an Excellence Fellowship.</p>

<p><strong><span class="attention"><span style="color: #ff0000;">For the application to be considered, each student has to fulfill the enrollment procedure as described on the</span> <a href="https://www.unige.ch/immatriculations/en/conditions" target="_blank">Admissions office website</a>.</span></strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>if you are a student at the UNIGE</strong>: a scan of your UNIGE card</li>
	<li><strong>if you are not yet a student at the UNIGE</strong>: the mail entitled &ldquo;UNIGE-Candidature: D&eacute;p&ocirc;t de votre dossier&rdquo; that you received after registering for the master&#39;s program (proof that you have begun the registration process) is required.</li>
</ul>

<p>The application (in English or French) must include the following documents in the form of a <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong> <span class="attention">SINGLE</span> </strong></span> pdf file:</p>

<ul class="type1">
	<li>A scan of your national identity card or passport.</li>
	<li>A Curriculum Vitae (2 pages maximum).</li>
	<li>The transcript of exams passed during the bachelor education, with all grades.</li>
	<li>A motivation letter in which you describe your specific scientific interests and your motivation to pursue a Master of Science program at the University of Geneva.</li>
	<li>Letters of references from two professors attesting your aptitude to this program.</li>
	<li>Any other pertinent information like results of GRE (General requirement examination), of TOEFL or other language test.</li>
</ul>

<p>The application is made <!-- <a title="Application" lang="en" href="https://formulaire.unige.ch/outils/limesurveyfac/sciences/index.php/289872?lang=en" target="_blank"><strong>online</strong></a> -->online, from the beginning of January of each year. After registering, you will receive an e-mail containing a personal link allowing you to fill out the application form.</p>

<p>Next application deadline is <span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong> <span class="attention">28 February, 2026&nbsp;</span></strong></span>(for Masters starting September 14, 2026).</p>

<p><span style="color: #ff0000;"><strong><span class="attention">IF ALL THE ABOVE MENTIONED CLAUSES ARE NOT COMPLETED, THE APPLICATION FILE WOULD NOT BE CONSIDERED.</span> </strong></span></p>

<p>Additional information is available at <a href="https://www.unige.ch/sciences/en/enseignements/formations/masters">http://www.unige.ch/sciences/Masters.</a></p>
