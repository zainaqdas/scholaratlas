Only the TOEFL iBT is recognized; other TOEFL formats are not accepted for application purposes. (Doctoral applicants are not eligible to take the TOBB ETÜ English proficiency examination.) Applications to Doctoral Programs: Applicants must hold a thesis-based master's degree. ALES Score Type: All ALES score requirements are based solely on the Quantitative score type. Scores from other ALES categories will not be considered. Scholarship Amounts for the 2025–2026 Academic Year Program Tuition Fee (₺) Master's 33.000 PhD 38.000 Students Who Passed the Doctoral Qualifying Examination 43.000 Special Achievement Scholarship This scholarship is awarded to a limited number of students who satisfy the eligibility requirements set forth in the relevant provisions of the regulations and are selected by the Rectorate. Includes either a monthly living stipend or a partial living stipend together with housing support. For recipients of the Special Achievement Scholarship, the monthly living stipend is calculated at 150% of the amount awarded to full-scholarship students. Applicants for the Special Achievement Scholarship in graduate programs must meet the following minimum requirements: ALES score: 85.000/100.000, Foreign Language score: 75.00/100.00, Undergraduate GPA: 3.00/4.00 or higher Scientific Achievement Scholarship This scholarship is awarded to students who receive scholarships from TÜBİTAK-BİDEB or other official funding institutions recognized by the Council of Higher Education (YÖK). Students holding this scholarship are exempt from tuition fees. Research Scholarship Consists of a full tuition waiver. Project Scholarship This scholarship is awarded to students employed in externally funded research projects conducted at the University. If the project is completed before the student's program ends, continuation of the tuition waiver shall be evaluated by the Institute Administrative Board upon the recommendation of the project coordinator. Staff Scholarship This scholarship is available to University employees. 50% Scholarship Consists of a 50% tuition waiver. General Conditions Students holding Full Scholarships, Special Achievement Scholarships, or Scientific Achievement Scholarships are expected to contribute to the University's research and teaching activities. Students holding other scholarship statuses (e.g., Research Scholarship, Project Scholarship, or 50% Scholarship) are required, upon request by the relevant Institute, to assist with educational activities such as proctoring undergraduate examinations and grading assignments.
