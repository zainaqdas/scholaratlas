Overview This research grant-in-aid, established in 2004, aims to support outstanding, self-funded international students pursuing degrees at the University and to promote the enrollment of exceptional international students. Recipients are awarded a monthly research grant-in-aid of 200,000 yen for the standard period of study in their respective graduate programs (up to one year for research students). In the academic year 2025, 44 new recipients were selected, bringing the total number of graduate students receiving the grants to 110. As of March 2026, a total of 952 individuals have been awarded the research grant-in-aid since the inception of the fellowship. Donations to The University of Tokyo Fellowship <FU Delin Scholarship> This scholarship, established through the generosity of Ms. Fu Delin, a philanthropist and descendant of the Fu family, whose lineage can be traced back to the Chinese Ming Dynasty, has a distinguished history. Ms. Fu has long personally supported Chinese and Japanese students studying abroad. Since 2017, she has graciously extended her philanthropy to provide financial assistance to international students seeking enrollment in graduate programs at the University of Tokyo. While this scholarship generally adheres to “The University of Tokyo Fellowship Implementation Guidelines on Special Scholarships for International Students,” it is primarily intended to support international students who are citizens of the People’s Republic of China. Note: Effective from the 2025 academic year, the scholarship is no longer being granted to new recipients. <KOGA Shinsuke Scholarship> Following his graduation from the University of Tokyo, Shinsuke Koga has enjoyed a distinguished career in global finance. Having himself benefited from international study, earning a Master of Business Administration from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Mr. Koga established the Koga Shinsuke Scholarship in 2016 to support graduate students from the United States and the United Kingdom studying at the University of Tokyo. This scholarship, while adhering to “The University of Tokyo Fellowship Implementation Guidelines on Special Scholarships for International Students,” is specifically intended for international students who are citizens of the United States or the United Kingdom.
