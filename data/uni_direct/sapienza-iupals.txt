Its purpose is to provide scholarships that will allow students residing in the Palestinian Territories to pursue their studies in Italy, beginning with the 2025/2026 academic year. Scholarships awarded by Sapienza: 2 What the scholarship includes: exemption from university fees, accommodation, meals, health insurance costs. Application deadline: June 30, 2025 Degree programs taught in English for which applications can be submitted: • Classics (Bachelor's Degree L-10) - Faculty of Arts and Humanities • Global Humanities (Bachelor's Degree L-42) - Faculty of Arts and Humanities • Business Sciences (Bachelor's Degree L-18) - Faculty of Economics • Economics and Finance (Bachelor's Degree L-33) - Faculty of Economics ANNOUNCEMENT - 31 July, 2025 The ranking list is published.
